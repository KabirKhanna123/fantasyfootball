Monte Carlo Draft Simulation

Runs the full 10-team, 16-round draft 100,000 times (fresh randomness each run — auto-pick jitter for every non-strategy team, keepers locked in every run). For each of the 160 draft slots, shows the top 3 players who landed there most often and what % of the 100,000 runs each one did. This takes 1-3 minutes depending on your device.
🔒 = this team's keeper for that round (100% every run, not a probability)
